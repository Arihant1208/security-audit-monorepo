### Status Values

- **Open** — Vulnerability identified, not yet assigned
- **Assigned** — Owner assigned, work not started
- **In Progress** — Remediation underway
- **Implemented** — Fix deployed, pending verification
- **Verified** — Fix verified by security team
- **Closed** — Remediation confirmed complete
- **Accepted** — Risk formally accepted (see acceptance log)
