# Compliance Framework Mappings

Maps audit checklist items to industry compliance standards so findings can be reported against specific frameworks.

## Supported Frameworks

| Framework | Document | Focus |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| [OWASP Top 10 Mapping](owasp-top-10.md) | Web application vulnerabilities → checklist items |
| [NIST Cybersecurity Framework](nist-csf.md) | Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover |
| [CIS Controls Mapping](cis-controls.md) | Critical security controls → checklist items |
| [SOC 2 Mapping](soc2-mapping.md) | Trust service criteria → audit evidence |

## Usage

After completing the audit checklists, use these mappings to:

1. **Report against a specific standard** — Map your findings to the compliance framework your organization follows
2. **Identify coverage gaps** — Find compliance requirements not covered by your audit
3. **Prioritize remediations** — Compliance violations may have different priorities than pure technical risk
